Legal Services

Every legal service property needs.

Six core services covering the full legal lifecycle of any real estate transaction in Rwanda — from initial due diligence to final title transfer.

🔍
Title Verification

Full NLA title deed verification, encumbrance searches, and ownership chain analysis. Know exactly what you're buying before any money moves.

Due Diligence
📋
LAIS Searches

Direct integration with Rwanda's Land Administration Information System. Pull official land records, cadastral data, and registered charges — digitally and traceably.

Government Integration
📝
Conveyancing

End-to-end conveyancing for property sales, transfers, and leases. Drafted in line with Rwandan property law, reviewed by a verified Rwanda Law Society member.

Transfer & Sale
💍
Marital Consent Checks

Mandatory under Rwandan law for all property held in community of property. We verify and obtain marital consent documentation before any transaction proceeds.

Compliance
🏢
SPV Formation

Special Purpose Vehicle structuring for fractional ownership and tokenisation projects. We create the legal entity that investors actually own a share of — with full title backing.

Tokenisation
🔗
Smart Contract Audit

Legal review of smart contracts governing token issuance, escrow release triggers, and ownership transfers. Ensuring every on-chain event has a legally binding off-chain counterpart.
