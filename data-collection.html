Getting Started

Ready to start?
Here's exactly how.

1
Download KoboToolbox Free
Go to kobotoolbox.org and download the app for Android or iPhone. You can also use KoboToolbox directly in any web browser — no download required. The app works offline, which is ideal for field work in areas with limited internet.
2
Create a free KoboToolbox account
Sign up at kobotoolbox.org — it's completely free. Once you're registered, note your KoboToolbox username. You'll need it to link your contributions to your Imali.biz profile and claim your rewards.
3
Open the Imali.biz data collection form
Click the "Open the Form" button on this page. To use offline: in the KoboToolbox app, tap Get Another Form and enter the form code, or scan the QR link. Download it before heading out so it works without signal.
4
Go out and record
Open the form, walk to an asset, and fill it in. Your phone will capture GPS coordinates automatically. Add a photo if you can — it helps verification. You can save a draft and continue later. Offline submissions sync automatically when you reconnect.
5
Link your accounts to earn rewards
Use the card on the right to enter your KoboToolbox username and your Imali.biz email. This links your submissions to your profile so every record you make counts towards your trust score, badges, and future token rewards.
🔗 Link Your Accounts
Connect your KoboToolbox account to your Imali.biz profile so your data contributions earn you trust score and rewards.
We store only your email and KoboToolbox username — nothing else. Your submissions will be matched to your profile automatically.
