Reducing vs Flat Rate

Reducing balance means interest is calculated on the outstanding loan only — you pay less as you pay down the principal. Flat rate charges interest on the full original amount throughout the term, often costing significantly more overall.

📈

Rwanda Mortgage Tips

Most Rwandan banks require a minimum 10–20% down payment for residential mortgages. A higher down payment reduces your monthly cost and typically secures a better interest rate. Loan terms of 10–20 years are most common.

💡

Extra Payments Save Thousands

Even a small extra monthly payment of 5–10% above the minimum can cut years off your loan and save you a substantial amount in interest. Use the Extra Monthly Payment field above to model the impact.
