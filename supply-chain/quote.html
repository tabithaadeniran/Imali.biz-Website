Quoting Tips
💡Quote competitively against the platform's pricing history to improve your match rate.
📦You don't need to quote on every item — partial quotes are accepted.
⏱️Realistic delivery dates improve your supplier trust score.
🔐Once accepted, payment is held in escrow and released per delivery milestone.
What Happens Next
1️⃣Project owner reviews your quote alongside others.
2️⃣If accepted, the order appears in your Dashboard and Delivery Tracker.
3️⃣Deliver materials and upload proof-of-supply photos at each milestone.
4️⃣Get paid from escrow as milestones are verified.
